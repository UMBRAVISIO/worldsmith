You have found the drowned forest... where the water keeps everything it is given.

Step carefully. The water is quiet tonight. It is not always quiet.

Do you see them... the little lights? They are not fireflies, and they are not lost. The lantern-bearers carry them... slowly... round and round the swamp.

They were here before the swamp. They will be here after.

Do not call out to them. They know your voice... better than you do.

And do not follow them past the old trees. The water between the trunks is deeper than it looks... and colder than it should be. Those who wade in... find the bottom much sooner than they expect.

Listen. The frogs have gone quiet. That means the lantern-bearers are near.

If a light drifts close... hold still. Let it look at you. It is only deciding... whether you will be here tomorrow.

Stand in the mist... breathe with the water... and when you leave, walk softly. The swamp remembers being walked.
