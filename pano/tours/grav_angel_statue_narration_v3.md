# Stone Angel — Guided Tour v3 (single-take; trimmed to fit seed's 120s limit in ONE call)
# Goal: consistent voice, gothic pacing via ellipses. ~590 chars.

You have found it... the old churchyard. Where the fog keeps its promises.

Stay... a while. The dead do not mind visitors. They get so few.

There she is... The Stone Angel. Two hundred years of rain, and frost, have worn her face smooth. But she still watches. She has never... once... blinked.

Count the votive candles... if you can. The number changes. It always changes.

But mind the hour. When the bell tolls... the angel turns her head. Only a little. Only once. And if you ever hear her wings unfold... stand very still.

She is only deciding... whether you are the mourner... or the mourned.

The candles know you are here now, wanderer.
