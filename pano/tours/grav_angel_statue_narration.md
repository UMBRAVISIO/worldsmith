# Stone Angel — Guided Tour Narration (caretaker voice)
# Voice direction: deep, slow, ominous — a mournful caretaker. Long pauses.
# Est. runtime ~90s at deliberate pace. ~940 chars → ~$0.11 (elevenlabs-tts-v3).

You've found it. The old churchyard... where the fog keeps its promises.

Stay a while. The dead don't mind visitors — they get so few.

There she is. The Stone Angel. Two hundred years of rain and frost have worn her face smooth, but she still watches. She has never once blinked.

Count the votive candles at her feet... if you can. The number changes. It always changes.

Listen. The wind through the yew trees carries names. Old ones. Whisper one back, and the fog will part just enough to let you pass.

But mind the hour. When the bell tolls, the angel turns her head — only a little, only once. And if you ever hear her wings unfold...

...stand very still. She is only deciding whether you are the mourner... or the mourned.

Welcome to the churchyard, wanderer. The candles know you're here now.
