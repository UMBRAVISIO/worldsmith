You have found the old churchyard... where the fog never lifts, and the candles never go out.

Stay on the path... the ground here does not remember being walked.

There... a light. One candle on a stone plinth. Someone tends it. No one ever comes.

The fence behind you keeps more than the living inside... or is it the dead? In this fog... who can say.

Mind the stones. Some lean. Some listen.

If you look past the great bare tree... you may see it. The chapel. One window glows. No one has held a service there in a hundred years... yet the window is always warm.

Look up... past the branches. The moon is thin tonight. It does not like to be watched.

When the fog parts... and you can see the gate... that is your moment to leave. The graveyard will still be here.

It always waits.
