# Stone Angel — Guided Tour v2 (gothic rewrite; pacing via punctuation, not spoken cues)
# NOTE: v1 delivered as female with Southern accent (ElevenLabs default). v2 attempts gothic
# delivery via clipped phrasing + heavy ellipses. Candidates A (elevenlabs) + B (seed-audio).

You have found it... the old churchyard. Where the fog... keeps its promises.

Stay... a while. The dead do not mind visitors. They get so few.

There... she is. The Stone Angel. Two hundred years of rain... and frost. Her face worn smooth. But she still watches. She has never... once... blinked.

Count the votive candles at her feet. If you can. The number changes. It always... changes.

Listen. The wind through the yew trees carries names. Old names. Whisper one back... and the fog will part... just enough... to let you pass.

But mind the hour. When the bell tolls... the angel turns her head. Only a little. Only once. And if you should ever hear... her wings unfold...

...stand very still. She is only deciding... whether you are the mourner... or the mourned.

Welcome to the churchyard, wanderer. The candles know... you are here now.
